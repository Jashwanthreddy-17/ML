    What the models learned

      Graphs and statistics

      Generated by backend/visualize.py from the trained models and the 2022–2023 test set. Select a chart to enlarge it. The SVG files are in figures/.

      How it works

      1. Data. IPL ball-by-ball deliveries, giving player-innings.
      2. Target. innings_runs: runs credited to the batter in one innings. Extras are excluded and zero scores are kept.
      3. Features. Career average, strike rate, recent form (last 5 and last 3 innings), consistency and days since the last match, all computed from innings before the one being predicted, plus innings, venue and teams.
      4. Models. Ridge (L2 shrinks all coefficients) and Lasso (L1 also zeroes out weak features), compared with a mean baseline.
      5. Validation. Chronological split, so the models never see future matches during training.

      Numeric features

      Each is built from earlier innings only.
